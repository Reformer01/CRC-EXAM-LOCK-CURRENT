# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Students at CRC taking high-stakes, proctored Google Forms exams. Many are first-time test takers: anxious, easily panicked by ambiguity, and unsure what counts as a violation. They sit with the extension in fullscreen for up to two hours under supervision.

Invigilators (CRC staff) manage exams from Google Sheets: they start and monitor sessions, review violations, clear false positives, unlock locked students, and adjust exam durations, sometimes while an exam is live.

## Product Purpose

CRC Exam Lockdown proctors Google Forms exams: a fixed countdown timer, fullscreen lockdown, violation tracking (tab switches, copy/paste, developer tools, shortcuts), and a Google Sheets backend that records sessions, violations, and admin actions, and emails violation reports. Success looks like exams completed fairly with zero lost submissions, students who understand the rules and do not panic, and invigilators who trust and can act on the data.

## Brand Personality

Steady, Serious, Protective. The voice of a firm-but-fair invigilator: consistent, professional, no games, clearly on the student's side of a fair exam. Calm in the face of student stress; decisive on integrity.

## Anti-references

- Surveillance / big-brother dashboards: no red-alert walls, blinking warnings, or implied guilt. Students feel supervised, not accused; admins are never screamed at by their own tools.
- Consumer gamified flash: no emoji-heavy playful chrome or candy gradients on a serious exam surface.
- Generic admin grayness: the admin side has craft too, just restrained.

## Design Principles

1. Calm authority: the interface never panics, and the student never wonders whether they are being accused.
2. Zero ambiguity: rules, consequences, and system state are legible before and during the exam.
3. Firm but fair: supervision is visible but never hostile; system interruptions are never blamed on the student.
4. Trustworthy data: invigilators act on evidence they trust, with clear audit trails and idempotent actions.
5. Restraint: serious surface, no flash; every word and pixel earns its place.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA as the target: 4.5:1 contrast, labeled fields, aria-live regions for violation and status announcements, visible focus states, reduced-motion respect. Students may be screen-reader users or color-blind; never make color the only signal.
